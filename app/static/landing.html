02 FROM DOCUMENT TO DECISION

Answers with a traceable path.

Each step connects a question to the source material and leaves room for people to review what comes next.

  1. 01Ingest

    Upload an approved document.

  2. 02Retrieve

    Find relevant passages in indexed knowledge.

  3. 03Reason

    Form a response to the question.

  4. 04Cite

    Return evidence with page references.

  5. 05Review

    Route sensitive requests for human decision.

Evidence remains visible. Approval records a decision; it does not carry out the requested action.
